# How to Sign a PDF on iPhone

Someone sent you a PDF to sign, and you only have your phone. You can sign it right there, with your finger, for free. There are two ways.

1. **Apple's Markup**, built into the Files app and Mail. Free, and already on your iPhone.
2. **PDFlow**, the app I built. Also free for signing. It keeps your original file and puts the other PDF jobs in the same place.

**Quick answer:** Open the PDF in the Files app. Tap the Markup button (the pen tip). Tap **+**, then **Add Signature**. Sign with your finger, tap **Done**, drag the signature into place, and tap **Done** again.

## Sign a PDF on iPhone with the Files App (Free) {#files-app}

This is the way most people need. It costs nothing and needs no app.

1. Save the PDF to the **Files** app, then tap it to open it.
2. Tap the **Markup** button. It looks like a pen tip in a circle.
3. Tap **+**, then **Add Signature**.
4. Sign in the box with your finger, then tap **Done**. Your iPhone keeps the signature for next time.
5. Drag the signature to the line. Drag a corner to make it bigger or smaller.
6. Tap **Done**.

One thing to know: Files saves the signature into that same file. The unsigned copy is gone. If you might need the blank form again, duplicate it first (press and hold the file, then tap **Duplicate**).

## Sign a PDF Attachment in Mail (Free) {#mail}

If the PDF came by email, you can sign it without saving it anywhere.

1. Open the email and tap the PDF attachment.
2. Tap the **Markup** button.
3. Tap **+**, then **Add Signature**, and sign or pick your saved signature.
4. Drag it into place, then tap **Done**.
5. Mail offers to reply with the signed PDF attached. Send it.

### Step 2: Tap Markup & Sign

The document opens on its history. Tap **Markup & Sign**. If the PDF has form fields, you will also see **Fill Form**, so you can type your name and date into the fields first.

### Step 3: Draw your signature once

Tap the signature button at the top, then **Create Signature**. Sign inside the box with your finger and tap **Save**. PDFlow keeps it for next time, so you only do this once.

### Step 4: Place it on the page

Tap the signature button again, then **Place Signature**. It lands low on the right of the page, where most signature lines are. Drag it onto the line. On iOS 26 you can also resize it with its handles. To sign another page, move to it with the arrows at the top and place it again.

### Step 5: Tap Done and send it

Tap **Done**. PDFlow saves a new version called **Marked Up**. Tap the share button to send it by Mail, save it to Files, or AirDrop it.

## Frequently Asked Questions {#faq}

### Can you sign a PDF on your iPhone?

Yes. Open the PDF in the Files app, tap Markup, tap +, and tap Add Signature. You sign with your finger and drag the signature into place. It is free and built into iPhone. PDFlow can sign a PDF too, and keeps your original file.

### How do you sign a PDF on iPhone with your finger?

In Apple's Markup or in PDFlow, you draw your signature in a box with your fingertip. Both apps save it, so you draw it once and place it again on every document after that. An Apple Pencil is not needed on iPhone.

### How do I fill and sign a PDF on iPhone?

If the PDF has form fields, tap a field and type. In PDFlow, open the document and tap Fill Form, then Markup & Sign. If the PDF has no fields, use Markup to add your signature, and add text with Markup's text tool.

### Is a signature drawn on iPhone a legal electronic signature?

It is a picture of your signature, placed on the page. Many people accept that for forms, leases and permission slips. Some ask for a signed-in e-signature service with an audit trail instead. If they sent you a link to one, use their link. If you are not sure, ask the person who sent the PDF.

### Is my signature uploaded anywhere?

Not by PDFlow. It saves your signature as an image inside the app on your phone, not in iCloud and not on a server. The signed PDF only leaves your phone when you send it.
