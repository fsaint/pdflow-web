## What Type of PDF Lock Do You Have? {#types}

PDFs can have two different types of password protection — it's worth knowing which one you're dealing with:

### User Password (Open Password)
A password required just to **open** the file. This is the most common type — you see it on bank statements, HR documents, and legal PDFs. If you have this password, PDFlow removes it in one step.

### Owner Password (Permissions Password)
A password that restricts what you can **do** with an open PDF — printing, copying, editing, etc. The document opens without a password but certain actions are blocked. PDFlow can remove owner password restrictions when you have the correct password.

### What PDFlow Cannot Do
PDFlow cannot crack or bypass passwords you don't know. If you've lost the password to a PDF you own, the most reliable path is to contact whoever sent you the document and request an unlocked version.

## Frequently Asked Questions {#faq}

### Can I unlock a PDF on iPhone without knowing the password?

No reliable method exists for removing a PDF password without knowing it — and attempting to do so on documents you don't own may violate copyright or confidentiality laws. If you own the document and have lost the password, contact the original sender or issuer to request an unlocked copy. PDFlow requires the correct password to unlock.

### Why do bank statement PDFs have passwords?

Banks password-protect PDF statements as a security measure. The password is typically your date of birth, account number, or a combination defined by the bank — check your bank's help documentation. Once you unlock the PDF with PDFlow, you can save a clean copy for use with accounting software or tax tools.

### Does removing a PDF password affect the content?

No. Unlocking a PDF removes only the access restriction — all text, images, tables, formatting, and annotations remain exactly as they were in the original file.

### Can I re-lock a PDF after unlocking it on iPhone?

Yes. PDFlow includes a separate [Protect tool](/protect-pdf-iphone) that adds a new password to any PDF. Unlock the file, make your changes, then re-protect with a new password — all in the same app.

### Is it safe to unlock PDFs on iPhone?

With PDFlow, yes. PDFlow processes files entirely on your device — your PDF is never uploaded to any server. This is important for sensitive documents like bank statements, tax forms, or legal files. Online PDF unlocking tools require you to upload your document to their servers, which carries privacy risk.

### Why does the password work on my computer but not on my iPhone?

Some PDF passwords are case-sensitive. Check that Caps Lock is off and that you're entering the password exactly as it was given to you. Also note that some PDFs use special characters that may be affected by iPhone autocorrect — turn off autocorrect before typing the password.
