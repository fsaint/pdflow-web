## Does iPhone Have a Built-In Page Delete Tool? {#built-in}

Yes. Since iOS 16, Apple's built-in Preview viewer lets you delete individual pages from a PDF without any third-party app. It works well for simple, single-page deletions on short documents.

Limitations of the built-in method:
- Shows pages in a **horizontal strip** — hard to navigate long PDFs
- **Overwrites the original file** — no separate export
- **One page at a time** — no multi-select for batch deletion
- No visual confirmation before saving — changes are immediate

For removing one or two pages from a short PDF, the built-in method is fine. For anything more — multiple pages, longer documents, or when you need to keep the original — PDFlow is the better tool.

## How to Delete PDF Pages on iPhone Using iOS Preview {#ios-preview}

### Step-by-step

1. Open the **Files** app and locate your PDF
2. Tap to open the PDF in Preview
3. Tap the **page indicator** at the top (e.g., "4 of 10") to open the thumbnail strip
4. Touch and hold the page thumbnail you want to delete
5. Tap **Delete** from the menu that appears
6. Repeat for any other pages you want to remove
7. The changes save automatically — there is no separate save step

**Important:** The iOS Preview method overwrites the original file. If you want to keep a backup, duplicate the PDF in the Files app before deleting pages.

## Frequently Asked Questions {#faq}

### Can I delete pages from a PDF on iPhone for free?

Yes. iOS 16 and later includes a built-in page delete function in the Preview viewer inside the Files app — no third-party app required. The limitation is it works one page at a time and overwrites the original file. PDFlow adds multi-select, grid view, and clean export as a one-time purchase with no subscription.

### How do I delete multiple pages from a PDF on iPhone at once?

The built-in iOS Preview tool does not support multi-select — you delete one page at a time. PDFlow lets you tap multiple page thumbnails to select them all, then delete in a single action. This is significantly faster for removing several pages from a long document.

### How do I remove blank pages from a PDF on iPhone?

Open the PDF in PDFlow and switch to the thumbnail grid. Blank pages are easy to spot visually. Tap each blank page to select it, then tap Delete. This is much faster than scrolling through a horizontal strip in the iOS Preview viewer.

### Will deleting pages affect the rest of the PDF?

No. Removing pages does not affect the content, formatting, or quality of the remaining pages. Text, images, and annotations on all other pages remain exactly as they were. PDFlow removes pages losslessly.

### Can I delete pages from a scanned PDF on iPhone?

Yes. PDFlow handles scanned (image-based) PDFs the same as text PDFs — you're removing page containers, not re-processing content. Page deletion works on any PDF regardless of how it was created.

### Can I undo a page deletion on iPhone?

In the iOS Preview built-in method, deletions save automatically and cannot be undone easily. In PDFlow, the original file is never modified — PDFlow always exports as a new file — so your original is always safe. If you delete the wrong pages, re-import the original and start again.
